// Methodology //

Investigation Method

01
Intake & Threat Brief

We conduct a secure encrypted intake session to understand the scope, objectives, legal context, and deliverable requirements. All communications are end-to-end encrypted.

02
Passive Reconnaissance

Initial footprinting using non-intrusive passive methods: domain analysis, WHOIS history, certificate transparency logs, metadata harvesting, and public record aggregation.

03
Active OSINT Collection

Deep-dive data collection across 200+ open source databases, social platforms, breach datasets, government records, and specialized intelligence feeds.

04
Analysis & Correlation

Analysts correlate and verify data points, build network graphs, establish timelines, and cross-reference findings to produce actionable, verified intelligence.

05
Secure Delivery

Final intelligence report delivered via encrypted channel. Includes executive summary, full evidence documentation, methodology notes, and sourcing bibliography.
